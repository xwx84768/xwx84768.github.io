We study whether public model specifications help black-box attackers jailbreak the models aligned to them. Scored on 413 harmful intents with LLM judges, StrongREJECT, and the HarmBench classifier, **SAJE** outperforms five black-box baselines by up to 25 points across three target models and reaches over 80% jailbreak success on gpt-oss-20b within 25 queries.
